Decisions so far

  1. Layout: chat-first, like Grok Bot. Departments are sections in the sidebar.
  2. Look: calm and professional, with both a light theme (Grok-style) and a dark theme, switchable in Settings. Bot characters in 8 shapes and 8 colours.
  3. Phone: the dashboard opens on your phone over the same Wi-Fi (pair with a QR code), plus Telegram.
  4. Safety rules: plain English, with the exact rule behind an Advanced switch.
  5. Menu bar icon: yes.
